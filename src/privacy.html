Privacy Policy

We handle your details the way we handle your hair — with care, and never shared around. Last updated: 1 January 2026.

1. Who we are

Iron & Blade Barbershop, Shop U12, 88 Rivonia Road, Rosebank, Johannesburg, 2196. We are an information "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What we collect

  • Booking details — name, phone number, email address, service, barber, date and time, and any notes you add.
  • Contact messages — name, email and message content you submit through the contact form.
  • Usage data — anonymous, aggregated analytics about how visitors use the website (pages visited, device type). We do not profile you or sell analytics data.

3. How your booking data is stored on this website

This website is a static site. When you book, your booking is stored only in your own browser's local storage so we can show you your upcoming appointments on the same device. It is not transmitted to a server, and clearing your browser data removes it. The appointment calendar file you download goes straight to your own calendar app; it is not stored by us.

4. Why we process it & the legal basis

We process personal information solely to run the business: to confirm and manage your appointment, send you a confirmation, respond to enquiries, and improve the shop's service. Processing is necessary to perform the booking contract you enter into with us (POPIA, s.11(1)(a) and (b)). Marketing emails are sent only where you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell personal information. We share it only where necessary to deliver your booking: our front-of-house team to seat you, and providers we use to operate (email hosting, appointment tools), each bound by confidentiality. We do not disclose your data to third parties for their own marketing.

6. Retention

Booking records are kept for the period needed for tax, accounting and legal obligations (a maximum of 5 years for transactional records). Contact-form messages are retained for 24 months. You can ask us to delete your records earlier at any time.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may object to further processing. To exercise a right, email tsumbedzomatloga@gmail.com. We respond within 21 days.

8. Cookies

This website uses a single local-storage flag to remember that you've seen our welcome offer — it doesn't track you. We serve no third-party advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; Google processes the request under its own privacy policy for the purpose of delivering the font files.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Access to personal information is restricted to the barbers and staff who need it to run your booking. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

10. Contact & complaints

Privacy questions: tsumbedzomatloga@gmail.com. If you feel we have not handled your information lawfully you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (www.inforegulator.org.za) — please give us the chance to put it right first.
