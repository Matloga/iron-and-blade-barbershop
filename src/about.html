Our story

Iron & Blade is a small shop with a long queue. Here's how one chair in Rosebank became four, and how we've kept the same standard since day one.

Est. 2012

Started with one chair
and a promise

Themba Nkosi opened Iron & Blade in 2012 with a rented chair, a second-hand clipper and one rule he refuses to bend: finish everything by hand. No rushing, no assembly-line cuts — if the fade isn't right, it isn't finished.

Fourteen years later we're four chairs deep in the same Rosebank corner. What hasn't changed: the straight razor on every finish, the hot towel on every beard job, and the belief that a haircut should be a proper sit-down — cold drink included.

How we grew

A short timeline

2012

One chair opens

Themba rents a single chair at the back of a barbershop supplies store. Word spreads one fade at a time.

2016

The corner shop

Iron & Blade moves into its own space on Rivonia Road with two chairs and a proper waiting area.

2020

Four chairs

Sipho, Marcus and Junior join. Online booking launches so regulars never wait for a seat.

2023

Apprenticeship

A structured apprenticeship programme — two juniors every year, trained to our standard from day one.

2025

Touch-up guarantee

Every cut now includes free shape-up touch-ups within 14 days. Because craft is a commitment.

Today

Still small, still sharp

Four chairs, no assembly line. Book your spot — the kettle is on.
